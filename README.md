## Featured projects

### [Precision Agriculture WebGIS Platform Evaluation](projects/precision-agriculture-webgis/README.md)
**Tools:** ArcGIS Online · CARTO · MangoMap · Web mapping · Spatial visualisation

Evaluated three web-mapping platforms for precision-agriculture applications using the same farm-management theme. The project compared interface design, ease of use, analysis functionality, visualisation capability, collaboration, and suitability for dynamic or real-time data.

**Highlights**
- Created and evaluated interactive agricultural web maps across three platforms.
- Compared usability, geoprocessing and visualisation capabilities.
- Assessed suitability for dynamic sensor and IoT-oriented workflows.
- Developed platform recommendations based on user expertise and project requirements.

### [Remote Sensing Image Analysis & Landslide Monitoring Strategy](projects/remote-sensing-image-analysis/README.md)
**Topics:** Earth observation · SAR · LiDAR · UAVs · Multispectral imagery · Change detection

Explored trade-offs between spatial resolution, swath size and revisit period in Earth-observation systems, designed a multisensor strategy for monitoring an active landslide, and produced multiple remote-sensing image products.

**Highlights**
- Examined optical and microwave remote-sensing capabilities.
- Designed a monitoring approach combining ground-based, airborne and satellite sensors.
- Considered high-resolution imagery, LiDAR, radar, thermal and multispectral data.
- Produced true-colour, false-colour and other enhanced image-analysis outputs.

### [Geospatial Technologies for Precision Agriculture](projects/geospatial-precision-agriculture/README.md)
**Topics:** GIS · Remote sensing · Drones · NDVI · IoT · AI/ML · Robotics · Databases

Investigated how modern geospatial and digital technologies support precision farming, crop monitoring, resource management and sustainable agricultural decision-making.

**Highlights**
- Reviewed GIS and remote sensing for crop-health and soil monitoring.
- Examined UAV-based farm asset mapping and targeted input application.
- Explored IoT sensor networks for real-time farm monitoring.
- Assessed AI and machine-learning applications in yield prediction, classification and disease detection.

### [History & Evolution of GIS](projects/evolution-of-gis/README.md)
**Topics:** GIS history · Cartography · CGIS · ArcInfo · WebGIS · Geodesign · IoT · AR · AI

Reviewed the development of GIS from early spatial mapping and computer-based geographic information systems through commercial desktop GIS, WebGIS and emerging geospatial technologies.

**Highlights**
- Traced major technological and institutional milestones in GIS development.
- Examined the influence of computing, databases and the web on modern GIS.
- Explored emerging directions including augmented reality, AI, IoT and connected geospatial systems.
